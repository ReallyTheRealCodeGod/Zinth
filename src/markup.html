How Zinth works

  1. Press Play. You are hearing a whole song: intro, verses, choruses, a break, a drop. Every note of every layer is locked to one key and scale, so nothing ever sounds off.
  2. Roll until you smile. New track rerolls everything. Lock a layer in the mixer to keep it, hit its dice to reroll just that one. Moods pick scale, tempo and sounds for you.
  3. Play along. Number keys are chords, letter keys are notes, all in key. Sketch chords into the tray and send them to the verse or the chorus.
  4. Record your hook. Press Rec, play the letter keys over the looping section, and your melody replaces the generated one, snapped to the grid. Undo is always one press away.
  5. Shape and leave. Edit the arrangement, kits, the drum grid, patches and effects. Then export WAV or MIDI and finish it anywhere.
