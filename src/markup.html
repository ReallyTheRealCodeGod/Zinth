How Zinth works

Six songs to start from

Press one and it plays. Each is a finished song and it is yours from the moment it opens: reroll it, play over it, export it. 1–6 load them from this sheet.

  1. Press Play. That is a whole song, and every note of it is locked to one key, so nothing ever sounds off.
  2. Roll the dice until you smile. New track rerolls everything. Lock a tile to keep that layer, or hit its own dice. Moods pick the scale, the tempo and the sounds.
  3. Play along. Number keys are chords, letter keys are notes, and they all fit. Sketch chords into the tray and send them to the verse or the chorus.
  4. Record your hook. Press Rec and play over the looping section. Your notes replace the generated ones, snapped to the grid. Ctrl+Z is always there.
  5. Go deeper when you want to. Studio, in the top bar, opens the arrangement, the chord editor, the drum grid and every knob of every sound. Song, beside it, exports WAV, MIDI or stems.
